Question & answer / When bits collide

After inserting cat and dog into the eight-bit model, what does testing the absent word god report?

cat maps to [0, 1]; dog to [2, 7]. Set bits: [0, 1, 2, 7]. god maps to [2, 1], both set: probably present, a false positive. Set bits cannot prove insertion.

Question & answer / Check the whole move

Robot (15, 50), radius 5; obstacle (45, 50), radius 10. Required gap: 8. Can the robot move 8 units directly forward?

Current surface clearance is 30 - 5 - 10 = 15. Forward ends at (23, 50), leaving 22 - 5 - 10 = 7. This is below 8, so the model rejects forward and checks steering candidates. This is a teaching schematic, not robot software.

Question & answer / Predict a narrower line

Keep text and font fixed in the layout lab, then narrow the width. Which claim is justified?

Narrowing width can move words onto more lines and increase height. A line need not fill the available width. Pin a baseline and inspect measured deltas; short text may still fit on one line.
